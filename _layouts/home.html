--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} Screen 1a — the masthead home. Four bands: hero, curriculum, the five domains, recent writing — then the footer from the default layout. The curriculum sits second, directly under the hero, rather than in a column at the foot of the page. That was a deliberate change after the first build: in a 1fr column at the bottom it was the last thing anyone reached and half of each entry was a wrapped degree title. This layout owns the page-level grid and nothing else; every band is an include and every style lives in its own partial. `content` is deliberately unused. index.html carries front matter only — there is no page-specific body, and putting one there would split the home page across two files. {%- endcomment -%}
