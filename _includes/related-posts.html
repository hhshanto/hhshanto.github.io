{%- comment -%} Related posts for the current document, ranked in three passes. 1. documents sharing at least one tag — authored 2. the document's nearest neighbours in the — computed atlas, by TF-IDF cosine 3. documents from the same subdirectory — filed Authored beats computed beats filed, and the passes run in that order. Pass 2 is the change: `_data/atlas.json` already carries a `near[]` list of the three closest documents for every piece, computed by `.tools/embed.mjs` and read by Liquid at build time, and until now exactly one page on the site looked at it. Falling back to "same folder" meant relatedness was decided by where a file happened to be saved: the two physics posts pointed at each other, and `statistics`, `psychology` and `about` hold one document apiece, so those posts got no Related section at all. Pass 3 is kept rather than replaced. `atlas.json` is generated offline and nothing rebuilds it automatically, so a document written today is not in it until `.tools/embed.mjs` runs again. Without the fallback a new post would silently lose its Related section, which looks like a bug in this include rather than a stale index. The candidate pool spans every collection, not just the current document's. The atlas does not respect domain boundaries and should not — the Higgs post's second-nearest neighbour is a psychology piece, and that crossing is the most interesting thing the list has to say. Each card states which pass picked it. This site's instruments all show their working, and "related" is otherwise a claim the reader has no way to check. Nothing is emitted when no pass matches, so a document with no siblings does not grow an empty heading. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign max_related = 3 -%} {%- assign pool = '' | split: '' -%} {%- for d in site.data.domains -%} {%- assign pool = pool | concat: site[d.collection] -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- comment -%} Picks are accumulated as one delimited string because Liquid cannot append to an array.
