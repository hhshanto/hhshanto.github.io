{%- comment -%} Blank cells that finish a .hgrid's last row. {% include hgrid-fill.html count=5 cols=3 %} The hairline grid draws its rules by letting the container's background show through a 1px gap. That is why an incomplete last row is not empty but *grey*: with five tiles in three columns the sixth cell shows the divider colour as a solid block. These fillers paint it back to the page ground. `cols` must be one of the column counts a .hgrid- modifier actually uses, since the fillers are shown and hidden by breakpoint in _primitives.scss. Include it once per column count the grid can take. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign remainder = include.count | modulo: include.cols -%} {%- if remainder > 0 -%} {%- assign missing = include.cols | minus: remainder -%} {%- for i in (1..missing) -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endif -%}
